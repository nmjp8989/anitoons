HOW TO PLAY

A little adventure, at your pace.

Explore: tap objects and characters.

Combine: drag an item onto something that can use it.

Move: drag a character onto a usable seat or vehicle when the story allows it.

Prefer tapping? tap an item, then its target. Every drag action also works with a keyboard.

Use Tab and Enter to explore. Press Escape to cancel a selected item. Music starts when you press Play.
