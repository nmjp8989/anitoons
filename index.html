ILLUSTRATION REFINEMENT

Mouth shapes

v20 simplified mouth system: The main talking set is intentionally reduced to seven practical speech visemes: Closed, M/B/P, EE/teeth, A, O, U/pucker, and F/V. Extra emotional shapes are provided separately for Smile, Sad, and Laugh. Drawing goal: professional cartoon features, strong line weight, no raster face or image overlays. Consistent eyes, eyebrows, mouth and hair are individual SVG layers, not separately generated full portraits. Speech-motion playback uses hand-set cues; imported audio is not automatically transcribed or analyzed. Timing is scaled to the audio duration for preview.
